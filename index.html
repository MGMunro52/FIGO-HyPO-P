Understanding ovulation and HyPO-P
A brief patient guide

What is ovulation?

Ovulation is when an ovary releases an egg. The egg may enter a fallopian tube and, if fertilized by sperm, may result in pregnancy.

What is an ovulatory disorder?

An ovulatory disorder occurs when ovulation does not happen normally. It may happen occasionally or continue for months or years.

What problems can it cause?

Ovulatory disorders can change menstrual cycles and can make pregnancy more difficult.

Why HyPO-P?

HyPO-P organizes the many possible causes according to the hypothalamus, pituitary, ovary, or the separate PMOS category.
